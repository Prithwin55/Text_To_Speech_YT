Once upon a time, in a small village nestled between rolling hills, there lived a boy named Ramu.
He was known throughout the village for his honesty and hard work.

Every morning before sunrise, he would walk to the fields and tend to his crops.
He never complained, even on the hottest summer days.

One afternoon, while walking through the forest, he discovered an old tree with a golden coin glinting beneath it.
Without hesitation, he picked it up and gave it to the poorest family in the village.

That night, a kind spirit appeared in his dream and said, "Your generosity has touched my heart. Ask for anything you wish."
Ramu smiled gently and replied, "I only wish for happiness for every person in my village."

The spirit blessed him, and from that day forward, prosperity came to every home in the land.
